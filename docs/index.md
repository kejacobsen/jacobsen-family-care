# Jacobsen Family Care

*A caregiver coordination text service by Katie Jacobsen*

Jacobsen Family Care helps parents and caregivers, like grandparents and sitters, coordinate childcare by plain text message. No app to install.

## What you'll receive

- Replies to your shared family list (for example, "add milk" or "list")
- Caregiver handoff summaries, like nap times, meals, and pickup plans
- Schedule reminders for your household
