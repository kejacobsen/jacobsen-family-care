**Program description.** Jacobsen Family Care helps parents and caregivers coordinate childcare by text. After opting in, you will receive replies to your shared family list, caregiver handoff summaries (such as nap times, meals, and pickup plans), and schedule reminders for your household.

**How to join.** Approved participants opt in by texting JOIN to (855) 678-1875. You will receive a confirmation message after joining. Joining is optional and is not a condition of any purchase.

**Message frequency.** Message frequency varies based on how your household uses the service.

**Costs.** Message and data rates may apply. For questions about your text or data plan, contact your wireless carrier.

**How to opt out.** Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed. To rejoin, text JOIN again.
