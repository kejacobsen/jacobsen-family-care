# Privacy Policy

*Jacobsen Family Care, by Katie Jacobsen. Last updated October 20, 2026.*

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jacobsen Family Care (Katie Jacobsen) collects through its text message program and how that information is used.

## Information we collect

- Your mobile phone number
- The messages you send to the program, such as list items and caregiving notes
- Records of when you opted in (texted JOIN) or opted out (texted STOP)
- Basic delivery records, such as timestamps and message status

## How we use it

Your information is used only to run the program: replying to your messages, keeping your household's shared lists and notes, sending caregiver handoff summaries and reminders, and honoring opt-out requests. It is not used for advertising.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Information is processed only by the service providers needed to deliver messages and store data (Twilio for text messaging and Amazon Web Services for hosting), solely to operate the program. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

## Text messages

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. See our [Terms & Conditions](terms.md) for full program details.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Information is kept only as long as needed to run the program. You can ask to have your information deleted at any time.
